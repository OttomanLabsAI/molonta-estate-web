One estate, four ways in

The Mediterranean as it once was

That is the locals’ phrase for this bay, and the family has kept it true: a 1930s stone house restored around its original walls, terraces of 250-year-old olive trees, and the Adriatic at the foot of the garden.

The estate

Eleven acres, entirely yours

The house

Stone & cypress

Built in the 1930s by the Metković family and restored around its original walls. Handmade furniture, five-star comfort, and beds for up to eight — with room for two more on request in a separate apartment.

The water

Pool & hot tub

A 14 × 5 m infinity pool looking straight down the Adriatic, and a hot tub for five in the gardens.

The grounds

Gardens & play

Terraced olives, citrus and figs; a game room with home cinema and billiards, an outdoor gym, private parking and wifi throughout.

The sea

Your own way onto the Adriatic

The beach

Private beach

Sun-warmed stone platforms with sunchairs and a shower, a kayak for the bay — and a mooring if you arrive by boat.

The boat

Estate yacht

A skippered Prestige 34 motor yacht, exclusive to guests of the estate. Coves, caves and island days along the coastline.

The city

Dubrovnik by sea

Cruise beneath the Konavle cliffs, swim on the way, and dock at the Old Town port — the way the city is meant to be arrived at.
